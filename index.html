Ludovico Franco

Trumpet player, improviser and doctoral researcher. I work on sound environments and their relationship with human perception and urban architecture, treating improvisation as a way of inhabiting space rather than a way of describing it.

PhD researcher in the joint doctoral programme of the conservatories of Pescara, Ferrara, Trieste and Udine. Based in Italy.
