About

Ludovico Franco is an improviser, trumpeter and PhD researcher in the joint doctoral programme of the conservatories of Pescara, Ferrara, Trieste and Udine. His research explores sonic environments and their relationship with human perception and urban architecture, treating improvisation as a tool for engaging with space.

In 2026 he presented his research at the Siena Jazz Symposium and at the SPaMEC conference of the Conservatorio di Napoli, and designed and led a listening and improvisation workshop within a Musica Bacàn artistic residency.

As a performer he has appeared internationally in experimental and contemporary music contexts, from the Swiss Institute to the Auditorium Parco della Musica and the Donostiako Jazz Fest, and has worked with Stefano Battaglia’s Tabula Rasa Ensemble at the Accademia Chigiana. In 2025 he released three albums, including La Linea Obliqua and Open Field.
