About

Ludovico Franco is an improviser, trumpeter and PhD researcher in the joint doctoral programme of the conservatories of Pescara, Ferrara, Trieste and Udine. His research explores sonic environments and their relationship with human perception and urban architecture, treating improvisation as a tool for engaging with space.

In 2026 he presented his research at the Siena Jazz Symposium and at the SPaMEC conference of the Conservatorio di Napoli, and designed and led a listening and improvisation workshop within a Musica Bacàn artistic residency. From 2021 to 2023 he was artistic director of Ekstasis, a series of contemporary music concerts held in churches across Rome.

As a performer he has appeared internationally in experimental and contemporary music contexts, among them the Casa del Jazz, the Auditorium Parco della Musica and the Donostiako Jazz Fest, and has taken part in artistic residencies with Musica Bacàn under the direction of Dan Kinzelman. In 2021/22 Stefano Battaglia invited him to join the Tabula Rasa Ensemble at the Accademia Chigiana, with concerts at the Teatro dei Rozzi in Siena. In 2023 he took part in Tobias Koch’s Sonic Architecture at the Istituto Svizzero in Rome, in collaboration with Emiliano Maggi.

Alongside his research he works in large-scale productions. Since 2024 he has been a member of Elodie’s band, playing at the Stadio San Siro, the Stadio Maradona, the Concerto del Primo Maggio in Rome and the Arena di Verona. He has also performed with Robbie Williams at the 2024 X Factor final and with Achille Lauro at the Circo Massimo in 2025.

In 2025 he won first prize and the Best Composition award at the Jazz in the Park Competition in Cluj, Romania, with the band Jemma, and released three albums, including La Linea Obliqua and Open Field.
